# Release 10 Oct 2026

## New
- **Structure designer** (in the header, before Fact sheet and About). Build a structure block by block, layer by layer: HESCO, Corner HESCO, sandbags, door, floor, roof, window and ladder. Cost, build and trim time, seal, collapse rules, a 3D view, and a C4 / collapse test. Examples: Recon Tower, Shelter, Bunker. Save your own; the admin can publish them.
- **Place your own structures on the map**: pick one under the piece list, click its icon, then click the map. Entrances, windows, ladder, height, cost and build time come from the structure.
- **Modify structure**: select one structure, then Modify structure. Take blocks off with the hammer or put them back. You get a warning before more than the block falls. It opens as a bar above the map, with an Advanced mode.
- **Side view in 3D**, turned with R / Shift+R. Layer ▼ ▲ and Hide above. The 3D button switches back to the old flat view.
- **Copy as text** in the top bar, and **Save .txt** / **Import .txt** in the Open menu.

## Changed
- Threat check reads structures **block by block**: every block is climbed and blown on its own, a hole is a way in, rooms under a roof are walked in.
- Bremer walls: back-to-back Bremers count as the higher wall and still block after one is blown. A Bremer facing an entrance, window or the Recon ladder covers it.
- No more dropping into an entrance from a block right outside it.
- Collapse rules from game tests (Recon floor 2: 5 of 10 ground HESCO, Shelter roof: 6 of 12, roofs on corner HESCO: 2 corners). C4 takes the 3×3 squares around the charge.
- **Bunker** as in the game: 4 corner HESCO, 6 windows, entrance at the south side's second square.
- Recon Tower with its roof gone: floor 2 is an open floor attackers can climb to.
- The square in front of a structure's ladder must stay free.
- The **trim** button next to Recon Tower and Shelter is gone. Older designs with a trimmed one still work.
- Fact sheet: no more C4 numbers for whole buildings (each block counts).
- Safety: structures that come with a design are checked block by block.

## Fixed
- A hole closed with Modify now stays closed when the design is reopened.

Rules: **change needed** for saving structures online (new collections `structures` and `test_structures`). The exact rules are in `11_STRUCTURES.md`, section 11, "Firebase rules to add". Until they are added, saving structures online is refused; everything else works.
