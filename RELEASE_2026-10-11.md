# Release 11 Oct 2026

## New login
- Log in with Google (one click), or with email and password.
- New email accounts get a "check your inbox" link; the account counts once it's clicked. Google accounts are checked already.
- "Forgot password?" emails a reset link. Nobody needs to ask the admin.
- First Google login asks for a planner name; the real Google name is never shown.
- The account button opens "Your account": name, email, Change name, Change password (email accounts), Log out.
- "Delete my account" removes the login, your saved designs and settings. Comments stay.
- Fresh start: old name + password logins are retired. They see "Old login" and are asked to make a new account. Old designs stay on the old login.
- Simple sign-up bots are caught by a hidden form field.

## Haul it, redesigned
- The plan comes first: pick a vehicle (Ural, Kodiak, Z20 Lakota, MH-6, Humvee, always in that order) with trips and total cash, then the trips in order with a picture of the bed and a "Done" tick per trip.
- Free room on a trip (pallet spot, crate stacks, trunk slots) is shown with one-tap buttons to fill it.
- "Buy at spawn" list next to the trips.
- Pack list button: one block per player, with a Copy button each.
- New "Tune the load": a card each for Supplies, Ammo, Fuel and Mechanical with + / − (one pallet or one stack), "Minimal" amounts, spare bar for Supplies, and a Reset button.
- Trunk packing: a small amount over a full pallet rides in the trunk instead of an extra trip. Helicopter trunks carry nothing for the base.
- One cash total everywhere. Stingray shots are shown apart ("+ $5,000 / Stingray shot", paid at the emplacement).
- Settings in one box: levels, loading options, Pallets Priority, helicopters, team, haul priority (default Supplies, Ammo, Mechanical, Fuel).
- Repair reserve removed: spare Supplies do that job.
- Fact sheet: trunk sizes Kodiak 12 and Humvee 9.
- Game colours and icons for Supplies, Ammo, Fuel and Mechanical.

Rules: no change needed. (Firebase console: Google sign-in on, sign-up allowed, snurras.github.io authorized — already done on test.)
