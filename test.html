      About the planner

      Plan a WARDOGS base on the HESCO grid, see how attackers would get in, and work out exactly what to haul before you build.

      Design
      Place every buildable, stack HESCO, turn pieces and seal entrances. The FOB build area is shown on the map.
      Threat check
      Finds the easiest way in on foot, from a car or a Ural, with or without C4, and plays it as an animation.
      Haul
      Build, ammo, fuel and mechanical supplies, cash cost and the fewest trips per vehicle.
      Fact sheet
      Every game number and most rules the planner uses, kept true and up to date after my best efforts.
      Build
      The build order step by step on the map, with layers you can hide to see what goes first.
      Save & share
      Personal, shared and template designs, with comments and thumbs.
