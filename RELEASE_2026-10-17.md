# Release 17 Oct 2026

## Season 02 pieces (numbers are guesses until checked in game, marked ESTIMATE on the Fact sheet)
- **Large Shelter** (Structures): a 6×6 roof at H3 on 4 thin pillars. Open underneath: pieces up to H2 fit under it. Nothing can be built on a pillar's square. Take 2 pillars off with Modify structure and the roof falls.
- **MG Nest** (Emplacements): 1×1, H1. A standing gunner on a machine gun, with firing angles like the Talon.
  - Can go **inside** a Bunker, Recon Tower, Shelter or your own structure. **[ ]** while placing picks the floor or the roof.
  - **Snap to grid** (toolbar) puts it against the nearest window. Untick it to place the gun anywhere in its square; **Alt + arrows** nudge it. Greyed out for other pieces.
- **Wooden Step** (Walls & entry): a 1×1 wooden platform, 0.2 high.
- **Structure designer:** new **Pillar** part. The Large Shelter is in Examples.

## Fields of fire
- Hover an emplacement: a **fan** round it shows each direction in blue (free), amber (limited) or red (blocked). A thin pillar only blocks its own slice.
- The hover card gets an **All round** strip: cover below, roofs above, and the gun's lowest angle, all the way round.

## Threat check
- **C4 blast:** one charge takes every block in the 3×3 squares around it.
- **Collapse:** blowing enough of a structure brings its roof down.

## Modify structure
- Floors are called **Ground · Floor 2 · Roof**. The chosen floor is drawn over the structure on the map.
- No HESCO Large or Door under a roof on HESCO X Size (1.65 headroom).
- Entrances take the block you pick (the Bunker entrance picks HESCO Small for you). Sandbags can go across a widened entrance.
- Doors and Sandbags look right on the map; **R** turns a Door.
- **Restore:** Erase or right-click on a block you took off makes it as built again.
- A block taken off shows as a red square; any block can go there.
- **Block a window with Sandbags.**
- Corner HESCO is now called **HESCO X Size** (label HX); H1 / H2 labels on HESCO Small / Large.

## Elevation
- **Add elevation / Remove elevation** in the selection bar, with as few Bumper blocks as possible.
- A Bumper block stays up in the build order while more pieces need it.
- Brush raises pieces next to a raised piece when **Auto elevation** (renamed from Elevation Stacking) is on.

## Other
- Sandbags on the ground stand against one side (R turns them).
- Side view draws blocks added with Modify structure and closed entrances.
- Structures may block a ladder (game quirk; Fact sheet switch).
- Broken designs no longer break the page; bad pieces are left out and the message says how many.
- Haul it, Cash box: one line per supply type.
- After Clear, a reload still brings the old design back until you place something new.
- Esc leaves Side view. Build time with only the FOB shows 0:04.

Rules: no change needed.
