# Release 11 Oct 2026 (b)

## Find designs
- New page under **Tools**: search every shared design and template.
- Filters: name, maker or piece · Haul out (vehicle + max trips) · Must have emplacements and stations (tap again = must not have) · Protected against (on foot, car, Ural) · least C4 to blow in · build time · templates, shared, favourites or mine · hide designs saved before the last Fact sheet change.
- Every result shows the same 3D look as Side view; turn it with one click.
- Sort by most ★, fewest trips, fastest build, hardest to get in, or newest. "Almost" lists designs that miss one filter and says which.
- Click a design for a big 3D view and its numbers, with arrows to step through the results. **Open in Design it** loads it; **← Back to search results** returns with your filters kept.
- **Copy link** gives a direct link to a shared design.
- Filters are remembered on your device. Logged out, you see the templates.
- Trips assume every vehicle is unlocked (ⓘ explains it); Haul it uses your own levels.

## Favourites
- ★ any shared design or template. The ★ count is how many players keep it, and "Most ★" sorts by it.
- Your favourites are in the Open menu.

## New layout
- **Tools** row under Design it → Haul it → Build it: Find designs and Structure designer.
- Fact sheet and About on the right of the top bar.
- Open, title and Save together on the right of the Tools row. **Open ▾** lists Find designs…, ★ Favourites, Templates and My designs. **⋯** holds Delete, Make template (admin), Save as .txt file and Import .txt file.
- Saving as Shared tells you it's now in Find designs.
- The title field no longer offers to fill in your name and address.

## Accounts
- **My designs: at most 10** saved online. Accounts with more keep them, but can't add new ones until they delete some. Save as .txt to keep more.
- Changing your planner name updates it on your shared designs in Find designs.
- Delete my account also takes your ★ off other designs.

## Less load on Firebase
- The page no longer downloads every shared design at start-up. Each shared design has a small search card; the list is kept on your device and only changed cards are fetched.
- Published structures are kept on the device too.

Rules: **changed** (already done on test, the same rules cover live): `cards` and `test_cards` rules, plus the indexes `structures` and `test_structures` (visibility + updated). Rule text: doc 06, "Card rules".

**After release, once:** log in on the live page as admin → Tools → Find designs → **Make missing cards** (bottom of the filters, press twice). Designs shared before this update need it to show up in Find designs. It costs 1 read and 1 write per shared design and template.
