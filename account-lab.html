About this page

A test bench for the new planner login. It uses the planner's real Firebase project, so accounts made here are real and will work in the planner once the new login moves in.

  • Passwords and emails are kept by Google's Firebase login service, never in the planner's database or this page.
  • Forgotten passwords: Firebase emails a reset link. Nobody needs to help.
  • Email accounts must click a link in their inbox before they count. Google accounts are already checked.
